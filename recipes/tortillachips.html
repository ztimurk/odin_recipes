Baked Tortilla Chips

Description

Ingredients
  • 1 (12 ounce) package corn tortillas
  • 3 tablespoons lime juice
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
Steps
  1. Gather all ingridients. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C)
  2. Stack tortillas in layers of 5 or 6. Cut through each stack to make 8 wedges.
    Arrange wedges in a single layer on rimmed baking sheets.
  3. Combine lime juice and oil in a spray bottle or mister; shake until well mixed
    Spray the tops of the tortilla wedges until slightly moist
  4. Combine cumin, chili powderm and salt in a small bowl; sprinkle mixture over the chips
  5. Bake in the preheated oven for 7 minutes. Remove from the oven. Flip chips, then mist and season again.
  6. Return to the oven, rotating the pans and switching racks. Bake, checking often to ensure they don`t burn, until chips are slightly browned and crisp, 5 to 8 more minutes.
  7. Remove from the oven and cool slightly before serving. Chips will crisp up more as they cool.
  8. Enjoy with a side of salsa!
